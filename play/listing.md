## English (en-US)
Short: Compare five weather forecast models side by side, with unusual-weather alerts.
Full:
Tahazit compares five forecast models (ECMWF, GFS, ICON, UKMO, Météo-France) and shows their median and how much they agree.
• Daily agreement score based on model spread
• Storm alerts only when most models agree
• Live station observations (Israel Meteorological Service, and METAR worldwide)
• An evening-before alert (about 20:00 local time) when tomorrow looks unusually cold or hot for your area, compared with its normal climate
• 14 languages, units by region
An independent app, not an official warning service. Push notifications are sent only for unusual heat or cold.
Free, no ads, open source. Built by Elad Elimelech.
